Learn it, play it, collect it

Each game has two modes, so children can explore at their own pace before they're tested.

  1. 📖

    Learn

    Tap anything on the map to see its name, a fun fact and, for landmarks, a real photo.

  2. 🎮

    Play

    Riddles, quizzes and races against the clock. A wrong tap shows the right answer, so every go teaches something.

  3. 🛂

    Collect

    Win stamps and badges in your passport. Progress is saved on the device, ready for next time.

Two levels for every class

Switch level any time from the home screen. Learn mode is the same for everyone.

🐣 Junior

Junior Infants to 2nd class

  • Shorter games
  • Colour hints and answers to pick from
  • Names shown to help early readers

🦅 Senior

3rd to 6th class

  • Longer games covering the whole map
  • Riddles with no names given
  • Timed challenges with a best time to beat
