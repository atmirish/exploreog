➗ Play Maths

Learn it, play it, collect it

The same three steps in every game, in every subject.

  1. 📖

    Learn

    Tap, drag and explore at your own pace. Teachers can step through worked examples on the whiteboard.

  2. 🎮

    Play

    Quizzes, riddles and races against the clock. A wrong answer shows the right one, so every go teaches something.

  3. 🛂

    Collect

    Win stamps, stars and badges for your passport. Progress is saved on the device for next time.
