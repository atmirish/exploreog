Getting started

Nothing to install and nothing to sign up for. Open the site on the whiteboard, a tablet or a laptop.

1

Pick a level

Choose 🐣 Junior (Infants to 2nd) or 🦅 Senior (3rd to 6th) on the subject's home screen. You can switch at any time.

2

Teach in Learn mode

Every game opens in 📖 Learn. Use Next example ▶ to step through worked examples with the class.

3

Practise in Play mode

Switch to 🎮 Play. Children get instant feedback, and a wrong answer always shows the right one.

🐣 Junior: Stages 1–2

Junior Infants to 2nd class

  • Shorter rounds (6 to 10 questions)
  • Picture hints and colour clues
  • Answers to pick from, so no typing

🦅 Senior: Stages 3–4

3rd to 6th class

  • Longer rounds with harder questions
  • Riddles, typed answers and multi-step problems
  • Timed challenges with Start and Stop buttons

Classroom ideas

A few ways teachers use the games.

⏱️

Daily warm-up

Start maths with a 3-minute Mental Maths Sprint on the board. Press Stop to talk through a strategy, then Start again.

🏆

Beat the class record

Play Senior County Finder together once a week and try to beat the class's best time.

👫

Pairs on tablets

After Learn mode on the board, pupils play in pairs. One taps, the other checks, then they swap.

☘️

Gaeilge links

Use Cúpla Focal in Geography and Maths to bring Irish into other subjects: place names, numbers, shapes and sums.

🧁

Real-world maths

Scale a Bake Off recipe for the class, then bake it. Use GAA Scoreboard after a big match.

🛂

Passport goals

Set a target, such as three stars in five games this term, and check passports on a Friday.

Sample 20-minute lesson: Fractions (3rd class)

5 minLearn together. Open Fraction Pizzeria. Step through the examples on the board: ½, ¼, ¾ and 4/8. Ask: "Why is 4/8 the same as ½?" 5 minChildren lead. Pupils come up and add pepperoni to make a fraction the class calls out. 8 minPlay in pairs. Senior Play mode on tablets. Pairs note any question they found tricky. 2 minWrap up. Share the tricky questions and solve one together.
